# /devspeak:smalltalk

Start a Devspeak role-play session for the `friday-smalltalk` scenario with a randomly chosen persona.

1. Pick one persona at random from `sarah`, `marco`, `priya` (vary your pick across sessions rather than always defaulting to the same one).
2. Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scenarios/friday-smalltalk.md` and the chosen persona's file under `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/devspeak-coach/personas/`.
3. Follow the `devspeak-coach` skill's rules for the entire session. This scenario is low-pressure and non-technical — keep it light.

Begin now with the persona's opening line (in their own voice/style), then wait for the user's response.
