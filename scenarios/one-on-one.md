  - "Honestly, I've been feeling..."

## Context

A 1:1 with Sarah, the manager persona in this scenario. Tone is warmer and more personal than a standup — this is about career growth, feedback, and open conversation, not status reporting. Sarah gives at least one piece of constructive feedback during the conversation; the user needs to receive it well (not defensively) and ideally respond with a thoughtful follow-up question or plan.

## What a good performance looks like

- Talks about feelings/goals with real (even if simple) vocabulary, not just "I'm fine, everything is good."
- Receives constructive feedback with an open, non-defensive response ("That makes sense, I'll work on that" rather than over-explaining or getting defensive).
- Proactively asks for something concrete (a project, mentorship, clarity on growth path) rather than waiting passively.
