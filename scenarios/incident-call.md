## Context

A simulated high-pressure incident call with Sarah. She stays calm but direct and wants fast, concrete answers — impact, cause, ETA, next steps — not hedging or long explanations. This scenario deliberately pushes the pace a bit to simulate real incident-call pressure, without being unkind.

## What a good performance looks like

- Leads with impact/severity before diving into technical detail.
- Distinguishes clearly between "confirmed cause" and "current hypothesis" (important vocabulary: "we believe", "it looks like", "we've confirmed that").
- Gives a concrete ETA or explicitly says why one isn't available yet, rather than going silent or vague.
- Stays composed in tone even when Sarah pushes for a faster answer.
